Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

Ansanm Pou Ayiti (“APA”, “we”, “us”) operates the APA mobile application and related services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email, and any message you send through Contact Us.
  • Donation information — payment details are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers in the app.
  • Device and usage data — basic connectivity status to support offline mode and error handling.
  • Data deletion requests — email address and optional details when you request account or data deletion.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and provide support.
  • Process donations and issue receipts through Stripe.
  • Improve app reliability and security.
  • Honor data deletion requests as required by applicable law and app store policies.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate APA (for example, Stripe for payments and Firebase/Google Cloud for hosting and deletion-request storage). We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We retain contact and deletion-request records only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. Donation records follow Stripe and our accounting retention requirements.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. To request deletion, use our Data Deletion Request page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the APA app or at ansanmpouhaiti.com.
