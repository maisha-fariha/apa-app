Request Data Deletion

Google Play and privacy compliance

You may request deletion of personal data associated with your use of the APA app — for example, contact form submissions or donation-related records we hold.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at bonjou@ansanmpouhaiti.org with the subject line APA Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address you used in the APA app or when contacting us, and any details that help us locate your data (for example, your name or approximate date of a donation or contact submission).
  3. We will verify your identity and process eligible deletion requests within 30 days. We may retain certain records where required by law (for example, donation receipts for accounting).

Alternative

You can also submit a request through our Contact Us page on ansanmpouhaiti.com. Select a general inquiry and mention that you are requesting data deletion under the APA app privacy policy.
